Customer Support Policy — synthetic reference document for demonstration.
Standard delivery is three to five business days from dispatch.
Express delivery is one to two business days and costs an additional $9.95.
The returns window is 30 days from the date of delivery.
Items must be unused and in their original packaging to qualify for a refund.
Refunds are processed within five business days of the returned item being received.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method and cannot be redirected.
Orders above $2,000 require a supervisor to approve any refund.
Digital gift cards are non-refundable once the code has been revealed.
Damaged items reported within 48 hours of delivery qualify for a free replacement.
The customer is responsible for return shipping unless the item arrived faulty.
Order tracking becomes available within 12 hours of dispatch.
Address changes can only be made before an order enters the packing stage.
Loyalty points are credited seven days after an order is marked complete.
Subscriptions can be paused twice per calendar year at no cost.
Support is available from 8am to 8pm local time, seven days a week.
